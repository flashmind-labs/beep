### Added

- Desktop: a report in a session's chat has a Challenge button. It starts a
  fresh agent of the same kind, on the same machine and folder, that tries to
  prove the report's claims wrong in a throwaway copy of the work. The header
  shows it running, with Open to see its session, then how many findings it
  left.
- Desktop: its findings arrive as review drafts under the claims they dispute,
  marked confirmed (it ran something) or plausible (it read the code), with
  their evidence. They go with your next message, first, and the agent is asked
  to fix each or show why it is wrong.
- Desktop: a saved review comment's card has a × to remove it.

### Fixed
