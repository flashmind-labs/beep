### Added

- iOS: a chat's final reply in the report shape appears as a report, with an outcome badge, word count, reading time, a larger headline, and each section's item count. Checks show as passed, failed or not checked, and Needs you is a highlighted card. Report text reads at a narrow width, while code and tables use the full width. A long final reply without that shape also gets the report header.
- iOS: a report of 80 words or more has a Summarize button in its header. It asks the session's agent for a shorter version: the outcome and result, then at most three one-line points about what to decide or know. The summary takes the report's place in its card, and Original shows the full report again.
- iOS: a report sits on a lightly tinted card, so it stands apart from your own messages.
